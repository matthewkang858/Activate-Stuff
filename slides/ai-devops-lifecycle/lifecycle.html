How to read it: the eight DevOps stages run down the left side (Dev, stages 1–4) and back up the right side (Ops, stages 5–8), looping from Monitor to Plan. Each card gives what AI is changing at that stage, then the platform incumbent, the best-funded challenger and a niche pick, in that order. 1 Plan. Shift: from tickets written for humans to specs and goals that agents execute. Atlassian: Rovo Dev Agents turn Jira issues into code plans, code and PR reviews. AWS Kiro: spec-driven agentic IDE, GA in 2026 as the replacement for Amazon Q Developer. Tessl: spec-as-source platform; $125M raised at a reported ~$750M valuation. 2 Code. Shift: autocomplete to chat to delegated agents running in parallel. GitHub Copilot: coding agent turns issues into PRs; Agent HQ opens it to third-party agents; 50M users. Claude Code: whole-task coding agent; $2.5B run-rate by Feb 2026. Factory: Droids for coding, review, testing and migrations; $200M at $5B in Sep 2026. 3 Build / Review. Shift: review is the new bottleneck (Faros: median review time up ~5x). Copilot code review: 60M+ reviews, 10x growth in under a year. CodeRabbit: review layer across GitHub, GitLab, Azure DevOps and Bitbucket; $143M Series C at $1.5B. Graphite: stacked PRs, merge queue and the Diamond reviewer; acquired by Cursor in Dec 2025. 4 Test. Shift: from hand-scripted suites to autonomous platforms. Tricentis: agentic quality engineering on Tosca and Testim; ~$500M ARR in 2025. QA Wolf: coverage-as-a-service; AI writes Playwright and Appium tests that QA Wolf engineers maintain, with an 80% end-to-end coverage guarantee; $36M Series B. Momentic: plain-language end-to-end tests for developers; $15M Series A. 5 Release. Shift: approvals become system checks and rollbacks need no human. LaunchDarkly: Guarded Releases with automated rollback. ServiceNow: Now Assist drafts change risk assessments for change managers to verify; Autonomous Specialists, announced at Knowledge 2026, execute low-risk standard changes within configured guardrails. Octopus Deploy: Recovery Agent finds the root cause of failed deployments. 6 Deploy. Shift: pipelines and infrastructure written from natural language. GitLab Duo Agent Platform: GA Jan 2026 with credit-based pricing. Harness: AI DevOps Agent builds pipelines and diagnoses failures. Pulumi: the Neo agent plans, reviews and executes cloud changes under policy; AI agents overall drove 20% of all operations on Pulumi's platform by May 2026, up from near zero. 7 Operate. Shift: the agent runs the first minutes of triage; on-call supervises. AWS DevOps Agent: GA Mar 2026; up to 75% lower MTTR in preview. Resolve AI: autonomous SRE valued at $1.5B in April 2026, though TechCrunch put ARR near $4M at its Series A. Traversal: read-only causal-ML SRE; reported 38% MTTR cut at DigitalOcean. 8 Monitor. Shift: product telemetry, LLM evals and engineering telemetry converge. PostHog: LLM analytics and evals beside product analytics; the incumbent in its niche, not in monitoring overall. Dynatrace: agentic operations layer on Davis causal AI. Braintrust: eval-first platform that gates prompt and model changes in CI/CD; $80M Series B. Caveats: funding figures are reported rounds and customer names are company-stated; the DigitalOcean 38% MTTR figure is customer- or comparison-site-reported; older valuations (LaunchDarkly 2021, Harness 2022, Octopus 2021, Pulumi 2023, PostHog 2025) may be stale.
